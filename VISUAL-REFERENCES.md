# VITRUVIO 3D reference direction

User-supplied visual references, recorded 2026-11-14. The images remain in the design conversation; no third-party artwork is redistributed with the application.

| Reference | Applied direction |
| --- | --- |
| Visor concepts with broad windows | Four furnished mission cabins with panoramic observation windows and continuous interiors around the orbit. |
| Cinematic character menu | Sharp anatomical subject, softer living environment, restrained motion, a separate subject light rig and grounded shadows. |
| VITRUVIO olive monitoring screenshot | Preserve the ceramic/olive interface, grey anatomical context, sage normal selection and amber watch status. |
| Translucent anatomical tissue close-ups | Clean x-ray jelly volume, translucent centres, soft grey grazing contours and stronger response as the camera approaches. Avoid hard comic outlines. |
| Genoverge genetic research board, supplied most recently | Clear rounded glass-like volume, soft broad highlights, visible internal layers and an airy sense of depth. Apply its material qualities using VITRUVIO's colours; its blue branding does not replace the health-status palette. |

The medical renderer uses physical clearcoat on inspected anatomy and a soft Fresnel response. Camera distance continuously controls volume intensity and selection lighting, including manual zoom. The four-band vGPU palette is linearly interpolated for a subtle cel response. No full-scene transmission pass is used: the independent habitat canvas must remain inexpensive and visible through alpha compositing.

Health-status meanings, numeric readings and clinical rules are independent of these illustrative material effects.
